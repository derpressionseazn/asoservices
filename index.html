Sami Supreme

A comedic 16-bit beat-'em-up across six real Colorado landmarks — Garden of the Gods, Royal Gorge, Red Rocks, the Great Sand Dunes, Denver Union Station, and Maroon Bells — talking your way past an unreasonable softball team (or fighting them) on the road to Chicago. Fridge Brain

Take a photo of your fridge. AI tells you what you can cook. The model was trained. The API was written. The app was opened once. The fridge was photographed. Never used again.

cause of death: friction at step 3
⏳

Vanishing List

A to-do app where tasks self-destruct after 24 hours. The philosophy: if it wasn't urgent enough to do today, was it ever real? Built the UI. Realized we needed it for the app itself. Irony won.

cause of death: philosophical collapse
🌱

Plantalk

AI that names your houseplants and writes their little backstories. "Fernanda the resilient." "Gerald who asks for nothing." Had a demo. Had names. Had no users. Had no plants either, actually.

cause of death: no plants, no users, no path forward
🗣️

DadText

Duolingo-style streaks and exercises for texting your dad back within a reasonable timeframe. The streak idea was funny for about six minutes. Then the actual coding started. Then other things happened.

cause of death: got distracted. ironically, by dad texts.

About

Who is doing this

One person. Based in Chicago. Has a background in tax compliance, data engineering, and digital identity — which explains the precision of the project planning and absolutely does not explain why the projects keep getting abandoned.

The gap between "this is a great idea" and "this is finished" is well understood here. It is approximately the size of the Grand Canyon. Every project starts with a genuine belief that this one will be different. This belief is, statistically, incorrect.

And yet. Four things shipped now. The page you're reading is one of them. Momentum is building. Slowly. In the wrong direction sometimes, but building.

ASO Services LLC is technically a real company. This website is not its finest work. That might be the point.
